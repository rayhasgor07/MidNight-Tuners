The Mid Night Club

The legendary street racing club known as the "Mid Night Club" operated from 1987 until 1999, becoming one of the most notorious and respected street racing clubs in history. Racing at speeds up to 300 km/h in Italian exotics — the Lamborghini Countach, Ferrari Testarossa — and Japanese icons like the Nissan 300ZX, GTR, Toyota Supra, and Mazda RX7.
